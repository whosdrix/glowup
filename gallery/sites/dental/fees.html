Practice Plan

Many of our patients spread the cost with our Practice Plan. For £12.50 a month you receive two examinations and two hygienist visits a year, all routine x-rays, and 10% off any other treatment. Children under 18 are seen free of charge when a parent is a member. Please ask at reception for a leaflet.

Paying

We ask for payment on the day of each visit. We accept cash, cheques and all major debit and credit cards.

Missed appointments

We know that things crop up. Please give us 24 hours notice if you cannot come, so we can offer the time to somebody who is waiting. We may charge £20 for an appointment missed without notice.
