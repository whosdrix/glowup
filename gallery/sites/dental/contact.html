Please do not use email to cancel an appointment as it is not checked every day. Telephone us instead.

By car

From the Market Square take Church Street up the hill and turn left at the top into Marlow Road. We are the red brick house with the green door, on the right just after the post box. The car park is behind the practice - turn right into Tanner's Lane and it is the first gateway on the right. There are six spaces.

By bus

The number 14 from the bus station stops directly outside. Ask for the Marlow Road post box.

Access

There are three steps up to the front door. If you use a wheelchair or find steps difficult, please use the side entrance from the car park, which is level, and ring the bell. The ground floor surgery and the hygienist room are both step-free.

Comments and complaints

We hope you will be happy with your care. If something is not right please speak to Sue Whitcombe, our Practice Manager, who will do her best to sort it out straight away.
