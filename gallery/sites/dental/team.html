Meet the Team

Dr Alan Whitcombe BDS - Principal Dentist

Alan qualified in 1984 and opened the practice in 1991 with one surgery and one nurse. He has a particular interest in dentures and in looking after nervous patients. Away from work he keeps bees and is a very average golfer.

Dr Priya Raman BDS - Associate Dentist

Priya joined us in September 2008 after six years in a busy practice in the city. She enjoys treating children and has completed further training in root canal treatment. She sees patients on Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Karen Doyle - Dental Hygienist

Karen has been our hygienist since 1999. Patients tell us she is thorough but gentle. She is here on Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Fridays.

Sue Whitcombe - Practice Manager

Sue runs the practice and is usually the voice you hear when you telephone. If you have a question about your account, the Practice Plan or anything else, Sue is the person to ask.

Our nurses

Our dental nurses Gemma and Tracey are both fully qualified and have been with us for many years. Tracey also looks after reception on Saturday mornings.
