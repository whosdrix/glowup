Meet The Team

Three chartered physiotherapists and one very organised receptionist

Our physiotherapists

Everyone who treats you at Kestrel is a member of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy and registered with the Health and Care Professions Council.

Ruth Ainsley

Clinic Lead

Ruth qualified in 2001 and spent eleven years in hospital musculoskeletal departments before opening Kestrel in 2012. She has an MSc in manual therapy and a special interest in persistent back pain. She runs, slowly, with Tarnford Harriers.

Dan Okafor

Sports Physiotherapist

Dan joined in 2013. He has been pitch-side physiotherapist for Tarnford RFC since 2010 and looks after most of our sports injuries, running gait analysis and post-operative knees.

Marta Lindqvist

Physiotherapist & Pilates

Marta trained in Gothenburg and moved to Tarnford in 2009. She teaches our clinical Pilates classes and treats neck, shoulder and pregnancy-related pain.

Front desk

Jo Pritchard answers the phone, finds you an appointment and sorts out insurance paperwork. If you are claiming through a health insurer, give Jo your policy and authorisation numbers when you book and she will invoice them directly.
