Our Services

Everything we offer at the clinic on Brewery Lane

Physiotherapy

We treat back and neck pain, sciatica, frozen shoulder, tennis elbow, hip and knee pain, sprains and strains, arthritis and whiplash. Your first visit is a 45 minute assessment. We ask about your pain and what you need to get back to, examine how you move, tell you in plain English what we think is wrong, and start treatment. You leave with a short exercise plan by email. Follow-up appointments are 30 minutes, and most people need between three and six.

Sports injuries

Dan Okafor leads our sports injury work. He has worked pitch-side for Tarnford RFC since 2010 and sees a lot of hamstrings, ankles and shoulders. We will give you a stage-by-stage plan for returning to training, and talk to your coach if you would like us to.

Running gait analysis

A 60 minute session on the treadmill with slow-motion video from behind and from the side. We look at how you land and how your hips and knees move, then give you two or three things to work on. Bring the shoes you run in.

Sports massage

Deep tissue massage for tight calves, backs and shoulders, before or after an event or just because you sit at a desk all week. 30 or 60 minutes.

Acupuncture

Ruth and Marta are both trained in acupuncture for pain relief. It is used alongside physiotherapy, within a normal appointment, at no extra charge.

Clinical Pilates

Mat classes taught by Marta Lindqvist, a physiotherapist, with six people at most. Tuesdays at 18:15 and 19:15, Thursdays at 18:15. You need a one-to-one assessment before joining so that Marta knows your history.

Post-operative rehabilitation

Rehabilitation after hip and knee replacement, ACL reconstruction, rotator cuff repair and spinal surgery. We follow your surgeon's protocol and send them a progress report if they ask for one.

Home visits

If you cannot get to the clinic we can come to you, within five miles of Tarnford, on Wednesday and Friday afternoons.
