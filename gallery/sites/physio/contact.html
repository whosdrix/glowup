Finding us

The Old Maltings is the brick courtyard behind the Co-op on Brewery Lane. We are the blue shopfront on the left as you walk in. There are four free patient parking spaces in the courtyard, marked Kestrel, and a pay and display car park on Mill Street two minutes away. The clinic is all on the ground floor with a level entrance.
